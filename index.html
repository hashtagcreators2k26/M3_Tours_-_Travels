M3 Tours & Travels is a South India tour operator that plans comfortable, reliable and convenient journeys for families, couples, pilgrims, corporate teams and groups. From pilgrim circuits to beach holidays and Dubai, one team handles the tickets, stay, food and transport.

Our Mission

Safe, pleasant journeys with 24-hour genuine service and warm hospitality.

Our Vision

To be the travel partner people trust for every trip across South India and beyond.

Why choose M3 Tour and Travels?

  • Qualified, experienced guides
  • Packages include Balaji special entry ticket, A/c or Non A/c deluxe rooms and pure veg meals
  • Transport costs covered: tax, toll, permit, parking and driver batta
  • Rail, bus, air tickets and luxury car rentals handled under one roof
